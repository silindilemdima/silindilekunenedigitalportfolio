01 / The story

How it started.

Referral by
Candy

I first got introduced to Momo's Hair Corner through a referral from Candy. The client reached out with a hand-drawn idea from his partner and asked if I could turn it into a logo for their new venture.

From the first message, I could tell it was a kiddies hair salon. My role was to take a heartfelt drawing that carried a lot of meaning and simplify it into a brand identity that could actually work across signage, products, packaging, social media, and a website.

The brief became clear: the salon needed to appeal to mothers raising African princesses. Momo was imagined as a Tsonga girl, so the identity had to feel playful, proudly African, colorful, warm, and easy for children to recognize.

I asked for permission to break the drawing down because it was too much for a logo. From there, I gave myself a week to translate the idea into a cleaner, scalable brand system.

02 / Approach

One week, from a sketch to a scalable system.

01

Listen

Reviewed the hand-drawn idea from the founder's partner and asked permission to break it down — the original carried more than a logo could hold.

02

Translate

Distilled the sketch into a kid-friendly identity for mothers raising African princesses. Momo, imagined as a Tsonga girl, became the heart of the system.

03

Build

Designed a custom playful wordmark stacked with hair-tool icons — a comb, a hair dryer, the orange bow — and a colourful supporting pattern.

04

Apply

Rolled the system across packaging (Curl Clouds, Little Lushies, Curl Sprinkles), business cards, a Midrand billboard, signage and a kid-friendly booking website.

03 / Color system

Pulled straight from the logo.

Six joyful tones — royal purple anchors the system; the rest play across packaging, signage and digital. Colourful enough for children to recognise from across a room, balanced enough to stay on-brand at billboard scale.

04 / The mark

Momo, the heart of the system.

A custom stacked wordmark with each letter doubling as a piece of the salon — a comb above the M, a hair dryer through the R, the orange bow on Momo herself. The tagline "Creating smiles one curl at a time" runs underneath in the same playful multi-colour cadence.
