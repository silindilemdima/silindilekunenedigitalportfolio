00 / The story

Rooted in nature.

Creative direction
A refined identity for olive oil.

OILERS is a premium olive oil branding and packaging project focused on building a distinctive identity with a strong connection to nature, heritage and craftsmanship.

The visual direction pairs a deep olive green with a muted sage tone, creating a restrained palette that feels grounded and contemporary. The logo establishes the brand's visual foundation, while the bottle labels translate that identity into a practical product application.

The logo has been completed and the bottle labels approved. The wider project remains in progress as packaging applications and product presentation assets continue to develop.

02 / Bottle labels

Designed for the bottle.

The front and back bottle labels have been approved. The design balances brand recognition with the practical information required on product packaging.

03 / Colour system

Olive green, muted sage.

The project palette is intentionally limited to two core colours. Deep olive green gives the identity depth, while muted sage offers a softer natural contrast.
