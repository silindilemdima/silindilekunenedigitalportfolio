Nelo Pharmacy.

Where care meets the corner shop — a warm, herbal identity for a community pharmacy that wanted to feel less clinical and more neighbourly without losing a single ounce of trust.

01 / The story

From Nelomed to Nelo Pharmacy.

Founders chose
The circle. Recoloured.

Nelo arrived on the brief as "Nelomed" — a working name the founders had outgrown by the time we sat down. It read too clinical, too distant from the kind of pharmacy they wanted to run: one where the pharmacist knows your name and your grandmother's prescription off by heart.

The first move was a naming refinement — dropping the "med", keeping "Nelo", and pairing it with the simple, honest word "Pharmacy". A name a child could say, a name an elder could trust.

Three logo directions went up on the wall: a teal capsule wordmark, a cool blue mortar emblem, and a circular badge built around a pestle and a single leaf. The founders kept circling back to the third — but the blues and greens of the first round still felt like every other pharmacy down the road.

We kept the circle and rebuilt the palette from the ground up: apothecary green for the herbal heritage, remedy orange for warmth, sandstone for the paper bag the prescription rides home in. The result feels like medicine and a meal — care, in both senses of the word.

03 / Color system

Apothecary green, remedy orange.

Six tones away from the clinical blues of the rejected concepts. Green anchors the badge and the storefront; orange carries the warmth across cards, totes and packaging; sandstone and cream give the system a paper-bag honesty.

04 / The mark

A circle of care.

A circular badge built around a pestle and a single leaf — medicine and plant, prescription and remedy, science and ritual. The wordmark sits across the centre with "Pharmacy" set quietly underneath, so the badge reads first and the category reads second.
