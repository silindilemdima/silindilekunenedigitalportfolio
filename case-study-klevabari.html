00 / The story

The story behind KlevaBari.

Slogan
La S'phuma Khona.

Firstly, the word KlevaBari is an oxymoron, combining two opposing ideas: Kleva (smart) and Bari (less intelligent). You may be wondering how these two words fit together within the brand.

Where we're from in Soweto, the term "Kleva" is often used to describe someone who is streetwise, sharp, and able to navigate different situations with confidence. On the other hand, "Bari" is associated with the opposite type of person — someone who stays indoors, minds their own business, focuses on academics, and chooses a safer path through life.

We intentionally merged these two words because we believe that both characteristics have value. Growing up where we did, we learned that being streetwise and being academically minded can work together in your favour when applied positively. KlevaBari represents the balance between intelligence gained from life experiences and intelligence gained through education.

The journey of the brand began in 2013 when Morena Mabeta, the co-founder, first came up with the idea. At the time, another designer was tasked with developing the creative direction, but the concept remained only as sketches and never progressed beyond that stage.

Fast forward to 2023, when I was looking for a way to monetize my art and creative skills. I approached Morena with fresh ideas and a renewed vision for the brand. What started as a conversation soon evolved into a partnership, leading to me becoming the Creative Director of KlevaBari.

Today, KlevaBari stands as more than just a streetwear brand. It is a representation of where we come from, the lessons we've learned, and the belief that success comes from combining different forms of intelligence to create your own path. This philosophy is captured in our slogan, "La S'phuma Khona" — a statement of confidence, resilience, and determination. It reflects the mindset of finding a way forward, overcoming challenges, and making something of yourself regardless of where you come from.

01 / Approach

A decade in a notebook, a season on the streets.

01

Inherit

Morena Mabeta first sketched KlevaBari in 2013. The idea lived in notebooks for a decade — a name, a philosophy, no rollout. In 2023 I came in as Creative Director to translate the sketches into a wearable system.

02

Name

KlevaBari is an oxymoron on purpose: 'Kleva' (streetwise, sharp) fused with 'Bari' (bookish, safe, indoors). Two Soweto stereotypes wearing the same t-shirt — because both kinds of intelligence get you out.

03

Draw

A crown-and-dice front mark set in a bubbly, hand-lettered wordmark — playful enough for street, sharp enough for hustle. The back carries the storytelling: Khuphula Amasukisi ('pull up your socks'), illustrated with a character lacing his sneakers.

04

Colourway

Every drop lives in three colourways — Cocoa & Orange, Cream & Green, Ink & Blue — so the same graphic tells a different story on each body. La S'phuma Khona runs across the sleeves and hems as a signature.

02 / Colourway system

Three colourways, one story.

Every drop ships in Cocoa & Orange, Cream & Green, and Ink & Blue. The neutrals ground the garment; the accent colour carries the crown, the wordmark and the sleeve print — so the same graphic reads differently depending on which body it lands on.

03 / Typography

Bubble letters for the street, serif for the story.

Wordmark
KlevaBari

Hand-drawn bubble script — soft, generous, unmistakably Soweto. Paired with a hand-inked crown and dice to nod at both the hustle and the game.

Slogan · Sleeve
La S'phuma Khona
Also: Khuphula Amasukisi

Set in a heavy graffiti display for backs and sleeves — the storytelling face of the brand. Support copy runs in a clean mono so the meaning sits legible below the shout.
